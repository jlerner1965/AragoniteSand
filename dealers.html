Selecting a grade

Three grades of one material. Particle size decides the application; the chemistry does not change between them.

Bagged line

Retail merchandising

The 20 lb printed bag is the only shelf-ready format and is carried by aquarium retail. Three grades side by side read as a category rather than a single product.

The 50 lb bag is plain woven poly for back-of-house and volume. Bulk bags and bulk loads are not merchandised.
