Why this line sells

What your customer gets that they cannot get elsewhere

01

A lot code on every bag

The code on the back panel opens the lab analysis for that production run on this site: mineralogy, calcium carbonate, moisture, acid-insoluble residue, bulk density and the sieve distribution. Most substrate is sold on a claim. This is sold on a document.

02

The fines figure is published

Cloudy water after a first fill is the single most common substrate complaint, and it is fines, not grade. We screen them out and publish what is left, per lot. That is a return you do not process.

03

One pallet covers the category

Reef, mixed marine and cichlid customers are served from three grades of the same material. Mix them on one pallet, learn what your floor actually turns, and reorder to that.
