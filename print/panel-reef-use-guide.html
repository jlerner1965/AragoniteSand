Before Use

Rinse in small batches until the water runs mostly clear. Light cloudiness on day one is normal.

For marine, reef and Rift Lake cichlid systems. Raises pH and hardness; not for soft-water species.

Storage

Store dry and covered. Shelf life is indefinite when kept dry.
