What the material does

What aragonite actually does in a tank

01

It supports alkalinity, within limits

Aragonite dissolves more readily than calcite and releases calcium and carbonate as it does. That supports alkalinity in a tank that is pulling it down. It does not hold a tank at a fixed pH, and any product that promises one is overstating it.

02

Grain size sets behaviour

Fine sand looks best and suits sand-sifting fish, but it lifts under strong flow. Coarse stays put and lets detritus fall into the bed. There is no single correct grade. There is a grade that matches your flow and your livestock.

03

Fines are the real variable

Cloudy water after setup is almost always fines, not the grade. We screen ours out and publish what is left in each lot, so you know before the bag is open rather than after.
