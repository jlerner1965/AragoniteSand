Questions

Distributor questions

What is the minimum order?

One pallet. Grades and pack formats can be mixed on it. Bulk moves by the truckload, quoted separately.

Do you sell direct to end users?

No. This is a trade price list. Retail and farm customers are referred to a stocking distributor.

Can I mix grades and pack sizes on one pallet?

Yes, in full layers per grade. Volume tiers count every pallet on the order regardless of what is on it.

How is freight handled?

Prices are FOB Stockton. Freight is quoted per order, LTL for single pallets and truckload above. Delivered pricing is available on truckload volumes.

What analysis comes with a shipment?

A certificate of analysis for the production lot shipped. The same analysis is published on this site against the lot code printed on the bag.

Is this the same material as the industrial grades?

Yes. One aragonite, screened to different particle sizes and packed for different markets. The industrial line is sold through aragocorminerals.com.

Does aragonite raise pH?

It supplies calcium and carbonate and buffers alkalinity. It does not hold water or soil at a fixed pH, and we do not claim that it does.
