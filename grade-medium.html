Fit

Where the medium grade works, and where it is the wrong choice

Grain size should match your flow and your livestock, not your preference alone.

01

Mixed reef and fish-only marine

Holds its place under the flow a mixed reef runs, and still looks like a flat rather than gravel. The default for a tank that has to please both fish and corals.

02

Tanks with strong return flow

Where a fine bed would drift and bare the glass, medium stays down. Detritus still settles on top where a clean-up crew can reach it rather than sinking into the bed.

03

Not for sand sifters

Sleeper gobies, sand-sifting starfish and burrowing wrasses want a grain they can pass through their gills and mouths. If the tank is built around them, take the fine grade.

The other two grades

Fine grade

0.5–1.0 mm · 18–35 mesh · 90 lb/ft³

The reef grade. Settles flat and suits sand-sifting livestock. Needs gentle flow at the substrate.

Coarse grade

2.0–5.0 mm · 4–10 mesh · 88 lb/ft³

African cichlid and hard-water freshwater tanks, high-flow marine, and filter media where a larger particle is wanted.
