Applications

Markets specifying this grade

Aquarium · most specified

Aquarium and aquaculture

Provides a carbonate substrate that supports alkalinity in systems drawing it down, and a grain size matched to livestock and flow.

Agriculture

Agriculture and soil

Supplies calcium and carbonate to soil. Aragonite is softer and more reactive than calcitic limestone at the same particle size.

Feed

Animal feed

Supplies dietary calcium as carbonate. Sized for blending and for free-choice mineral feeding.

Turf · most specified

Turf and horticulture

Adds calcium carbonate to sand rootzones and potting media without the fines that impede drainage.

Water

Water treatment

Dissolves into low-alkalinity water passing through it, raising hardness and buffering capacity.

Aquarium selection

Selecting for an aquarium

Aquarium applications for this grade, and where another grade is specified instead.

01

General marine and mixed reef

Holds position under the flow rates typical of a mixed reef while retaining the appearance of a settled flat.

02

Rootzone and contact media

Drains freely in sand rootzones and passes water in contact vessels without the packing a fine grade shows.

03

Not for substrate sifters

Substrate-sifting livestock require a particle that passes the gill rakers. Specify fine.
