Fit

Where the fine grade works, and where it is the wrong choice

Grain size should match your flow and your livestock, not your preference alone.

01

Reef and sand-bed tanks

Settles into a flat, even bed with a clean line at the glass. The look most reef keepers are after, and the grade that shallow sand beds are built on.

02

Sand-sifting livestock

Suits gobies, sleeper gobies, sand-sifting starfish and wrasses that bury at night. Coarser grades are hard on gill rakers and on burrowing behaviour.

03

Not for high flow

Fine sand lifts and drifts under strong bottom flow, and it will pile against the rockwork. If your powerheads sweep the substrate, take the medium grade instead.

The other two grades

Medium grade

1.0–2.0 mm · 10–18 mesh · 92 lb/ft³

The forgiving grade. Holds against stronger flow and still reads as a natural flat. Start here if you are unsure.

Coarse grade

2.0–5.0 mm · 4–10 mesh · 88 lb/ft³

African cichlid and hard-water freshwater tanks, high-flow marine, and filter media where a larger particle is wanted.
