Privacy

This page explains what aragonitesand.com collects and why. It is operated by AragoCor Minerals LLC, 2453 Grand Canal Blvd, Suite 200, Stockton, CA 95207.

Quote requests

When you send a quote request we receive the details you enter (name, email, phone, company, delivery location, product interest and message), the page you sent it from, and your IP address for spam control. We use this only to answer your request and, if you become a customer, to fulfil orders. We do not sell or rent it. Requests are delivered to our sales inbox through our email provider and may be copied to our order-tracking system.

Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. It sets cookies and reports aggregated usage to Google. You can block analytics cookies in your browser settings without affecting the site.

Your rights

Email info@aragocorminerals.com to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. California residents have additional rights under the CCPA and can exercise them the same way.
