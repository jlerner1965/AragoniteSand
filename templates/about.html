Ownership

Who is behind the bag

AragoCor Aragonite is a product line of AragoCor Minerals LLC. AragoCor supplies natural oolitic aragonite from The Bahamas in bulk to farms, water utilities, glass makers and formulators, and has done so from Stockton since the company was formed. The aquarium sand is the same mineral, screened to three aquarium grades and put in a bag that carries its lot code.

The company is stated here and in the footer of every page because we think you should know who you are buying from. The parent site, aragocorminerals.com, carries the industrial product line, the technical data sheets and the company's contact details, which are the same as the ones on this page.

The Stockton operation

From ooid bank to your tank

01

Sourcing

The aragonite is a marine oolitic sand from naturally forming deposits on the Bahamian carbonate bank, sourced through Aragosan Minerals Limited. No quarry blasting and no calcination step. Source documentation is available to customers.

02

Washing and drying

Rinsed to take out salt and silt, then dried to a moisture figure we publish per lot. Moisture is what makes a bag heavy for nothing and clumps on the shelf, so it is measured, not assumed.

03

Screening

Screened in Stockton to the three grades: fine at 0.5–1.0 mm, medium at 1.0–2.0 mm and coarse at 2.0–5.0 mm. The fines below 0.25 mm that cloud a new tank are screened out and the remainder is reported on each lot's sieve analysis.

04

Bagging

Bagged in-house in 20 lb retail bags and 50 lb trade bags. Each production run gets a lot code printed on the back panel, and that code is what the lot lookup on this site answers to.

Testing practice

What a lot analysis is, and is not

Every production run is sampled after bagging and sent for analysis. The report covers mineral phase by X-ray diffraction, calcium carbonate content, moisture at packaging, acid-insoluble residue, bulk density and a dry sieve distribution. The sample date, the bagging date and the testing lab are printed with the results, and the results are published under the lot code on this site. Lab name and methods to be confirmed, see README

The analysis describes the sample that was tested on the date given. It is a measurement, not a warranty that every grain in every bag matches it. Specifications published elsewhere on this site are typical values across lots. Where the two differ, the lot analysis governs.

One thing we do not test for, because it cannot be promised: a pH value. Aragonite dissolves more readily than calcite and supports alkalinity in a tank that is pulling it down. It does not hold water at a fixed pH, and we do not say that it does.
