What the material does

What aragonite actually does in a tank

What to tell a customer at the counter, and what not to promise them.

01

It supports alkalinity, within limits

Aragonite dissolves more readily than calcite and releases calcium and carbonate as it does. That supports alkalinity in a tank that is pulling it down. It does not hold a tank at a fixed pH, and any product that promises one is overstating it.

02

Grain size sets behaviour

Fine sand looks best and suits sand-sifting fish, but it lifts under strong flow. Coarse stays put and lets detritus fall into the bed. There is no single correct grade. There is a grade that matches your customer's flow and livestock.

03

Fines are the real variable

Cloudy water after setup is almost always fines, not the grade. We screen ours out and publish what is left in each lot, so the answer is on the shelf before the bag is open rather than at your returns counter after.
