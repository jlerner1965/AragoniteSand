Why stock it

What the lot code does for a store

01

The spec sheet is on the bag

A customer asking what the sand is made of gets an answer with a date and a lab on it, not a paragraph from the packaging. Fewer returns from cloudy first fills, because the fines figure is published.

02

Three grades, one supplier

Reef, general marine and cichlid customers are covered from one pallet. The three grades are the same material screened differently, so the story at the counter is simple.

03

Bagged in California

Screened and bagged at our Stockton facility. Short lead times to the West Coast and Mountain states, and one company behind the material, the analysis and the invoice.
