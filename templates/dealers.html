Selling it

How the category sells at the counter

Three grades of one material. The whole conversation is grain size, and it takes about a minute.

01

Ask what lives in the tank

Sand-sifting gobies, wrasses and a smooth reef flat mean fine. A mixed reef with real flow means medium. African cichlids, hard water and diggers mean coarse. That single question sorts nearly every customer.

02

Then ask about flow

If the powerheads sweep the bottom, fine sand will drift and bare the glass. Move them up a grade. This is the returns conversation you would otherwise have next month.

03

Show them the lot code

The code on the back panel opens the lab analysis for that run on this site, including the fines figure that decides whether their water clouds. No other bag on your shelf can do that.
