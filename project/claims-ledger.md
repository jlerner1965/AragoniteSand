## Prohibited on this site (removed from the previous build or never to be added)

- "Supports stable pH" (reef bag mockup), "helps hold pH", "raises pH and hardness", guaranteed buffering or alkalinity.
- "Resist acidification", "helping the system…", "silica sand does nothing".
- "Gentle on gobies/wrasses/cucumbers", species safety, "safe for all species", "reef safe".
- "More reactive than calcite or crushed coral" as a performance claim (TB-OA-002 states higher relative solubility under comparable acidic conditions; the sand site links the brief rather than restating it as a benefit).
- "Live sand", biologically active, bacterial colonization, nutrient removal, denitrification, animal health, feed conversion, disease prevention.
- Dry density "about 90 lb/ft³" and the tank calculator (no controlled source).
- "Buy direct", "ship a few bags to hobbyists", retail language, "best", "purest", "premium".
- Carbon/climate/sustainability claims. The parent site's "sustainably sourced" line is not repeated here.
- Chemical-free, dust-free, impurity-free, heavy-metal-free, silica-free (silica is reported as <0.01%, which is how it is stated), food/feed/pharmaceutical grade.
- Any MOQ, lead time, bag count, pallet weight or pallet dimension.
