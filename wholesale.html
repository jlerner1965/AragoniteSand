Pallet net weight

Bags are printed with grade, grain size, net weight and the lot code. The lot code on every bag on a pallet is the same unless the pallet is mixed, in which case each grade carries its own.

Mixed-pallet policy

Any combination of the three grades in retail bags on one pallet, in full layers per grade. Mixing is allowed on opening orders and on reorders alike. Trade bags and retail bags can share a pallet in full layers.
