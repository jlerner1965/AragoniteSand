Application categories

Commercial applications for aragonite sand

One natural material, screened to different particle ranges and packed for different buyers. Each category below lists the property a buyer normally evaluates, the specifications that matter, what is needed to qualify an order and which documents are available. Final suitability is always determined for the specific formulation, process or system.

Categories

Category 1

Aquarium and aquaculture products

Substrate, graded media and carbonate source for marine, brackish and selected freshwater systems, supplied to distributors, brands, public aquariums and aquaculture operators.

Aquarium & aquaculture supply
Property evaluated
Particle distribution for bed, flow and cleaning; carbonate chemistry; fines and turbidity.
Relevant specifications
AG-CAL 0.2–0.5 mm, WT-CAL 1–3 mm, or a custom cut; typical chemistry; moisture.
To qualify
System type, species, chemistry targets, particle range, volume, packaging and destination; trial on a sample.
Documents
TDS-OA-001, grade sheet, lot COA, SDS, sieve analysis.
Category 2

Water treatment and alkalinity media

The 1–3 mm WT-CAL grade is published for process-water remineralization, alkalinity management, pond preparation and carbonate aggregate duty. Potable-water applications require the buyer to confirm the applicable standard before supply; no drinking-water certification is claimed on this site.

Water treatment at AragoCor Minerals
Property evaluated
Particle size for contactor flow and bed behaviour; carbonate content; dissolution under the process conditions.
Relevant specifications
WT-CAL 1–3 mm; CaCO₃ 96.17% representative; specific gravity 2.93; turbidity 380 NTU representative.
To qualify
Water chemistry, flow, contactor design, target pH or alkalinity, applicable standard, volume and destination.
Documents
TDS-WT-001, TDS-OA-001, lot COA, SDS.
Category 3

Turf, horticultural and landscape materials

The 0.2–0.5 mm AG-CAL grade is published for turf, golf-course and landscape top-dressing, greens and horticultural mixes, and the custom range for greens plating sand. Decorative or landscape-aggregate use of the coarser grade is quoted on enquiry against the buyer’s specification.

Turf and horticulture at AragoCor Minerals
Property evaluated
Particle range for the mix or top-dressing programme; calcium content; colour and roundness.
Relevant specifications
AG-CAL 0.2–0.5 mm or custom; elemental calcium 38.51% representative; sphericity 0.82, roundness 0.87.
To qualify
Programme or mix specification, soil or rootzone report where relevant, volume, packaging and destination.
Documents
TDS-AG-001, TDS-OA-001, lot COA, SDS, OMRI certificate where organic-input status is relevant.
Category 4

Specialty blending and formulation

Sized calcium carbonate for fertilizer blending, nutrient carriers, mineral blends and private formulations. Custom classification across 20–325 U.S. mesh is produced to a written target distribution. Feed-ration calcium is a custom classification subject to the ration formulator’s particle requirements and a lot-specific certificate of analysis; no feed-grade designation is claimed here.

Property evaluated
Target particle distribution, flow and blending behaviour, moisture, calcium content.
Relevant specifications
Custom cut or a published grade; typical chemistry; moisture 5–10% published range.
To qualify
Target specification or sieve limits, formulation role, annual volume, packaging and destination.
Documents
TDS-OA-001, lot COA, SDS, sieve analysis.
Category 5

Manufactured products and industrial evaluation

Custom classification is published for kiln feed, proppant feed and similar process inputs. Glass-batch feed (GL-CAL, below 0.1 mm) and functional filler (PL-CAL, to specification) are industrial grades handled through AragoCor Minerals rather than this sand site.

Industrial grades at AragoCor Minerals
Property evaluated
Particle distribution, chemistry, crystal form (orthorhombic aragonite), density, thermal behaviour.
Relevant specifications
Custom cut; full composition in TDS-OA-001; TB-OA-002 for aragonite vs. calcite comparison.
To qualify
Particle-size requirement, formulation or process conditions, annual volume, performance criteria, destination.
Documents
TDS-OA-001, TB-OA-002, lot COA, SDS.
Category 6

Private-label and distribution supply

AragoCor Minerals matches grade, sizing, packaging and private-label options to a requested volume. Labelling, bag artwork and branding limits are agreed per programme. Indicate private-label interest on the quote form and the programme terms are discussed directly.

Property evaluated
Consistency of grade lot to lot; packaging configuration; documentation for the buyer’s own labelling and compliance.
Relevant specifications
Published grade or custom cut; lot COA against contract limits.
To qualify
Market, bag sizes, annual volume, labelling requirements, destination or distribution points.
Documents
TDS, lot COA, SDS, source documentation.

Scope of this page. Categories are listed because the controlled documents publish the grade for that use or because supply is available on enquiry; listing does not mean each is an established market of a given size. No engineering, agronomic, environmental, animal-health or regulatory performance is claimed. Application rates and process performance are not specified by AragoCor. For calcium carbonate applications beyond sand, including glass, cement, fillers, energy and remediation, see the industries overview at AragoCor Minerals.
