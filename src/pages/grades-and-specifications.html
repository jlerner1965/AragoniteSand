Values are representative analyses or published ranges; guaranteed limits are agreed per contract and confirmed per lot by certificate of analysis.

How grades are defined

Every grade is screened and classified from one natural commercial gradation of oolitic aragonite. The chemistry does not change between cuts; the particle-size range does. A grade code therefore identifies a nominal size range and the applications it is normally supplied for, not a different mineral.

Two published grades cover most sand requirements. Where an application needs a different distribution, the material is classified to a written customer specification anywhere in the 20–325 U.S. mesh range.

Reading the status column. Representative analysis is a measurement from an identified sample and describes the material as tested. Published range is a historical deposit range for dried, unwashed material. Customer specification required means the range is set by the buyer’s written spec. None of these is a guaranteed minimum or maximum.

Processing

Mechanical collection, drying below 93 °C, screening and classification. No quarry blasting, no calcination, no chemical treatment, no additives at any stage (manufacturing process document PDS-OA-001). Store dry and covered; shelf life is indefinite when stored dry.

Intended-use guidance and limitations

Match size to the application
Particle distribution affects settling, suspension, flow, cleaning, packing, blending and interaction with equipment. Choose the range for the bed depth, flow, process or formulation, then confirm it on a sample.
Chemistry is condition-dependent
Aragonite is a carbonate mineral that reacts with acids. Any dissolution, and any contribution of calcium or alkalinity to a system, depends on the operating conditions and the particle size. AragoCor does not specify application rates or process performance.
Fines, moisture and turbidity
As-received moisture is a published range of 5–10%. Natural sand carries fines from handling; evaluate initial turbidity and material retention for the process or system it enters.
Confirm on a representative sample
Suitability for any particular process, system or formulation must be confirmed by the buyer on a representative sample under the intended conditions.
